# Sabina Azimova Dental Atelier — Tactile Paper & Storybook Web Experience

Official landing page and clinical showcase for **Dr. Sabina Azimova** (Dentist & Hygienist, Saint Petersburg).

## 🌟 Visual Identity & Design System
- **Tactile Cotton Paper Canvas**: Creamy handmade cotton paper background with subtle grain and natural organic shadows.
- **Handwritten Kid Notes & Doodles**: Expressive annotations (`Caveat` Google Font, full Cyrillic support), washi-tape corners, and doodle badges (*"Бережно & без слез для малышей и взрослых ✨"*).
- **Polaroid Photo Cards**: Clinical cases styled as authentic polaroid prints with unretouched macro photography.
- **Children's Smile Laboratory**: 4-step storybook adaptation journey and doctor's 2-minute brushing routine memo card.

## ⚙️ Technical Highlights
- **Swiss GBT AirFlow Protocol**: Glycine powder polishing & Piezon ultrasound scaling showcase.
- **Accessible Interactive Before/After**: Split-slider with mouse, touch, and full **WCAG 2.1 AA keyboard support** (`←`, `→`, `Home`, `End`, `PageUp`, `PageDown`).
- **WCAG AA+ Color Contrast**: Refined ink tokens and amber gold accents meeting strict 4.5:1 / 7:1 contrast ratios.
- **Bilingual Interface**: Instant zero-lag language switching between Russian (RU) and English (EN) with `localStorage` memory.
- **Direct Concierge Booking**: Integrated Telegram deep-link booking and in-page accessible status alerts (no native blocking `alert()`).
- **Pure Zero-Dependency Stack**: HTML5, Vanilla CSS3 Custom Properties, Vanilla ES6 JavaScript Engine.

## 🚀 Running Locally
Run `server.ps1` in PowerShell:
```powershell
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\server.ps1
```
Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.
